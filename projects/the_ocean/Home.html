Welcome to ENG240 Developers Hub!

Welcome to a community where engineering meets technology and software development. ENG240 Developers Hub is a platform created for engineering students who want to go beyond the classroom and develop skills in programming, web development, software engineering, and problem-solving.

We believe that engineers of the future should not only understand how things work but also know how to build, automate, and improve them using technology. Whether you're just starting your coding journey or already developing projects, this platform is a place to learn, collaborate, exchange ideas, and grow together.

Our goal is to help engineering students transform their ideas into real digital solutions while developing the technical and teamwork skills needed for the modern engineering world.
